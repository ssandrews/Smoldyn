# Filament nodes adsorbing to and desorbing from a surface.
#
# A flat membrane lies at z = 0, and filaments start above it, fluctuating. The red
# filaments have adsorb and desorb interactions with the membrane for all of their
# nodes (surface_interaction membrane front all adsorb 2 0.2 0.5, and
# surface_interaction membrane front all desorb 0.2 0.1). A node that comes within
# 0.2 of the membrane adsorbs at rate 2, and then moves within the membrane with
# lateral mobility factor 0.5, until it desorbs at rate 0.2 and is placed 0.1 above
# the membrane. Since nodes near adsorbed nodes are also held near the membrane, the
# red filaments tend to zip down onto it. The blue filaments only adsorb by their
# plus ends, and desorb faster, so they attach to the membrane by their tips and the
# rest of each filament fluctuates above it. Their plus ends have a larger capture
# distance (0.5) and faster adsorption (rate 10), so that they find the membrane. All filaments have a reflect interaction
# with the membrane, which keeps their free nodes above it.
#
# smoldyn adsorbDesorb3D.txt

graphics opengl_good

dim 3
boundaries x -20 20
boundaries y -20 20
boundaries z -5 15

time_stop 100

color both 0.6 0.6 0.6
polygon both edge
panel rect +2 -50 -50 0 100 100
end_surface

surface_interaction membrane front all adsorb 2 0.2 0.5
surface_interaction membrane front all desorb 0.2 0.1
end_filament_type

end_filament_type
