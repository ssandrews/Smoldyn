# A surface that moves in response to the forces that filaments exert on it.
#
# Filaments grow upward from a floor toward a lid, which has a repel interaction with
# them (surface_interaction lid front all repel 20 2). The lid feels the equal and
# opposite force, and since it has a mobility (mobility 0.2), the growing filaments
# push it upward. Red molecules are bound to the underside of the lid and move with
# it. Green molecules don't diffuse and start just above the lid, which pushes them
# along as it moves. Blue molecules diffuse, and the lid keeps them on their own side.
# Walls enclose the system, since the system boundaries don't affect molecules when
# there are surfaces, and the filaments have a reflect interaction with them. The lid
# is wider than the walls, so that sideways motion can't open a gap at its ends, and the
# bound molecules are only on its middle panel, which is inside the walls. The lid's force and total displacement are listed at the end.
#
# smoldyn movingSurface2D.txt

graphics opengl_good

dim 2
species bound pushed free
boundaries x 0 100
boundaries y 0 100

time_start 0
time_stop 60
time_step 0.01
random_seed 1
boxsize 10

frame_thickness 0

difc pushed 0
difc free 5
color bound(all) red
color pushed green
color free blue
display_size all(all) 3

start_surface walls
action all both reflect
color both black
thickness 1
panel rect +0 0 0 100
panel rect -0 100 0 100
panel rect -1 0 100 100
end_surface

start_surface floor
action all both reflect
color both black
thickness 2
panel rect +1 0 10 100
end_surface

start_surface lid
action all both reflect
color both 0.5 0.5 0.5
thickness 3
mobility 0.2
panel rect -1 -50 30 50 left
panel rect -1 0 30 100 middle
panel rect -1 100 30 50 right
end_surface

start_filament_type pusher
color purple
thickness 2
polygon edge
dynamics Euler
kT 0.1
standard_length 1
standard_angle 0
force_length 10
force_angle 10
mobility 1
elongation_rate 1
surface_interaction floor front all softwall 20
surface_interaction lid front all repel 20 2
surface_interaction walls front all reflect
end_filament_type

random_filament pusher:p1 3  15 11  1.571
random_filament pusher:p2 3  32 11  1.571
random_filament pusher:p3 3  50 11  1.571
random_filament pusher:p4 3  68 11  1.571
random_filament pusher:p5 3  85 11  1.571

surface_mol 30 bound(front) lid rect middle
mol 40 pushed u 30.5
mol 100 free u 20
mol 100 free u 65

cmd A diagnostics surface

end_file
