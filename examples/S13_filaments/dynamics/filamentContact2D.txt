# Filaments that push on each other when they come into contact.
#
# Filaments grow upward from a floor to a ceiling, which makes them buckle sideways. In
# the left lane, red filaments have a softwall contact interaction with each other
# (filament_interaction contact softwall 20), and they have a radius of 0.75 (radius
# 0.75). Thus, two segments push apart when their centerlines are closer than the sum
# of their radii, which is 1.5, and the same applies to two parts of one filament. Blue
# filaments in the right lane are the same, but without this interaction, so they pass
# through each other. The contact force has a limit, so a strong enough push can still
# force one filament through another, and filaments that have crossed in 2D stay crossed.
#
# smoldyn filamentContact2D.txt

graphics opengl_good

dim 2
boundaries x 0 80
boundaries y 0 40

time_start 0
time_stop 40
time_step 0.01
random_seed 5
boxsize 4

frame_thickness 0

start_surface walls
color both black
thickness 1
panel rect +0 0 0 40
panel rect +0 40 0 40
panel rect -0 80 0 40
panel rect -1 0 40 80
panel rect +1 0 0 80
end_surface

start_filament_type contact
color red
thickness 2
polygon edge
dynamics Euler
kT 0.1
standard_length 1
standard_angle 0
force_length 10
force_angle 10
mobility 1
radius 0.75
elongation_rate 1
elongation_max_length 60
filament_interaction contact softwall 20
surface_interaction walls both all reflect
end_filament_type

start_filament_type plain
color blue
thickness 2
polygon edge
dynamics Euler
kT 0.1
standard_length 1
standard_angle 0
force_length 10
force_angle 10
mobility 1
radius 0.75
elongation_rate 1
elongation_max_length 60
surface_interaction walls both all reflect
end_filament_type

random_filament contact:c1 3  15 1  1.571
random_filament contact:c2 3  20 1  1.571
random_filament contact:c3 3  25 1  1.571
random_filament plain:p1 3  55 1  1.571
random_filament plain:p2 3  60 1  1.571
random_filament plain:p3 3  65 1  1.571

end_file
