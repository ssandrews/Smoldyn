# Persistence length of thermally fluctuating filaments in 3D.
#
# This simulates three filament types that differ only in their bending force
# constants, and records all node positions so that persistence3D.py can measure
# their persistence lengths. The measurements can be compared with the persistence
# lengths that Smoldyn reports in its filament type output (run with -o, or look at
# the diagnostics), which are computed for the angle-biased chain model as
# Lp = 2 l kyaw kpitch / (kT (kyaw + kpitch)).
#
#   sym       force_angle 10 10 10    predicted Lp = 10
#   asym      force_angle 10 40 10    predicted Lp = 16
#   asymroll  force_angle 10 40 100   predicted Lp = 16
#
# The filaments start with random_segments, which draws the joint angles from their
# Gaussian equilibrium distributions, so they start at equilibrium and the initial
# printout can be compared with the later ones. The short time step is needed for
# accurate statistics with Euler dynamics; with a time step of 0.001, the measured
# correlations between neighboring segments are a few percent off.
#
# Typical results are Lp = 10.5, 16, and 15.5 for the three types, each with an
# uncertainty of about 0.3, so they agree with the predictions to within about 5%.
#
# smoldyn persistence3D.txt
# python3 persistence3D.py

graphics none
#graphics opengl

dim 3
boundaries x -20 20
boundaries y -20 20
boundaries z -20 20

time_start 0
time_stop 100
time_step 0.00025
random_seed 5

output_files persistence3Dout.txt
cmd i 0 100 0.1 printFilaments persistence3Dout.txt

start_filament_type sym
dynamics Euler
kT 1
standard_length 1
standard_angle 0 0 0
force_length 200
force_angle 10 10 10
mobility 1
thickness 1
color red
polygon edge
end_filament_type
