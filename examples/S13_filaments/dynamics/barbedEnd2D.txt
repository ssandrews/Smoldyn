# Barbed filament ends, which elongate more slowly when they are pushed against.
#
# The system is divided into two lanes. In each lane, three filaments grow upward from
# the floor and push on a lid, which has a mobility (mobility 0.2), and the
# applysurfaceforce command pushes down on both lids with the same constant load. The
# filaments in the left lane have open plus ends, which always elongate at rate 1. The
# filaments in the right lane have barbed plus ends (end_state plus barbed), which
# elongate at rate v=r+a*(exp(-k*F)-1), entered with elongation_rate plus barbed r a k.
# Here, r is 1, a is 1, k is 2, and F is the part of the surface force on the end node
# that is parallel to the end segment and pushes against the end. Thus, barbed ends
# elongate at rate 1 until they reach the lid, and then more slowly as the force on
# them rises, while open ends keep elongating at rate 1, so the open filaments end up
# much longer and more buckled than the barbed ones. Buckling is random, so the
# amounts that the lids rise vary from run to run. The lids' forces and total
# displacements are listed at the end.
#
# smoldyn barbedEnd2D.txt

graphics opengl_good

dim 2
boundaries x 0 66
boundaries y 0 100

time_start 0
time_stop 60
time_step 0.01
random_seed 1
boxsize 10

frame_thickness 0

start_surface walls
color both black
thickness 1
panel rect +0 0 0 100
panel rect +0 33 0 100
panel rect -0 66 0 100
panel rect -1 0 100 66
end_surface

start_surface floor
color both black
thickness 2
panel rect +1 0 10 66
end_surface

start_surface lid1
color both 0.5 0.5 0.5
thickness 3
mobility 0.2
panel rect -1 1 22 31
end_surface

start_surface lid2
color both 0.5 0.5 0.5
thickness 3
mobility 0.2
panel rect -1 34 22 31
end_surface

start_filament_type open
color red
thickness 2
polygon edge
dynamics implicit
kT 0.1
standard_length 1
standard_angle 0
force_length 10
force_angle 30
mobility 1
elongation_rate 1
surface_interaction floor front all softwall 20
surface_interaction lid1 front all repel 20 2
surface_interaction walls both all reflect
end_filament_type

start_filament_type barbed
color blue
thickness 2
polygon edge
dynamics implicit
kT 0.1
standard_length 1
standard_angle 0
force_length 10
force_angle 30
mobility 1
elongation_rate plus barbed 1 1 2
end_state plus barbed
surface_interaction floor front all softwall 20
surface_interaction lid2 front all repel 20 2
surface_interaction walls both all reflect
end_filament_type

random_filament open:a1 3   8.2 11  1.571
random_filament open:a2 3  16.5 11  1.571
random_filament open:a3 3  24.8 11  1.571
random_filament barbed:b1 3  41.2 11  1.571
random_filament barbed:b2 3  49.5 11  1.571
random_filament barbed:b3 3  57.8 11  1.571

cmd E applysurfaceforce lid1 0 -1.5
cmd E applysurfaceforce lid2 0 -1.5
cmd A diagnostics surface

end_file
