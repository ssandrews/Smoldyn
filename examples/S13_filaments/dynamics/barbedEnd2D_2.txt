# Barbed filament ends, which elongate more slowly when they are pushed against, and
# branching near a surface.
#
# This is like barbedEnd2D.txt, but with narrower lanes and a third lane. In each lane,
# three filaments grow upward from the floor and push on a lid, which has a mobility
# (mobility 0.2), and the applysurfaceforce command pushes down on all three lids with
# the same constant load. The filaments in the left lane have open plus ends, which
# always elongate at rate 1. The filaments in the middle lane have barbed plus ends
# (end_state plus barbed), which elongate at rate v=a*min(exp(-k*F),2)-b, entered with
# elongation_rate plus barbed a k b. Here, a is 1, k is 2, b is 0, and F is the part of
# the surface force on the end node that is parallel to the end segment and pushes
# against the end. Thus, barbed ends elongate at rate 1 until they reach the lid, and
# then more slowly as the force on them rises.
#
# The filaments in the right lane are barbed too, and they also branch, but only near
# the lid. The statement "surface_interaction lid3 front all branch 3 0.1" makes each
# node within distance 3 of the front of lid3 branch at rate 0.1 per unit time, while
# nodes farther from the lid don't branch. The branches are new filaments of the same
# type, so they have barbed plus ends, they push on the lid too, and they can branch
# when they are near the lid. The result is a branched network just under the lid,
# which shares the load among more filament ends. branch_min_spacing keeps branches at
# least 2 apart along each mother filament. The lids' forces and total displacements
# are listed at the end.
#
# smoldyn barbedEnd2D_2.txt

graphics opengl_good

dim 2
boundaries x 0 60
boundaries y 0 100

time_start 0
time_stop 60
time_step 0.01
random_seed 1
boxsize 10

frame_thickness 0

start_surface walls
color both black
thickness 1
panel rect +0 0 0 100
panel rect +0 20 0 100
panel rect +0 40 0 100
panel rect -0 60 0 100
panel rect -1 0 100 60
end_surface

start_surface floor
color both black
thickness 2
panel rect +1 0 10 60
end_surface

start_surface lid1
color both 0.5 0.5 0.5
thickness 3
mobility 0.2
panel rect -1 1 22 18
end_surface

start_surface lid2
color both 0.5 0.5 0.5
thickness 3
mobility 0.2
panel rect -1 21 22 18
end_surface

start_surface lid3
color both 0.5 0.5 0.5
thickness 3
mobility 0.2
panel rect -1 41 22 18
end_surface

start_filament_type open
color red
thickness 2
polygon edge
dynamics implicit
kT 0.1
standard_length 1
standard_angle 0
force_length 10
force_angle 30
mobility 1
elongation_rate 1
surface_interaction floor front all softwall 20
surface_interaction lid1 front all repel 20 2
surface_interaction walls both all reflect
end_filament_type

start_filament_type barbed
color blue
thickness 2
polygon edge
dynamics implicit
kT 0.1
standard_length 1
standard_angle 0
force_length 10
force_angle 30
mobility 1
elongation_rate plus barbed 1 2 0
end_state plus barbed
surface_interaction floor front all softwall 20
surface_interaction lid2 front all repel 20 2
surface_interaction walls both all reflect
end_filament_type

start_filament_type branched
color green
thickness 2
polygon edge
dynamics implicit
kT 0.1
standard_length 1
standard_angle 0
force_length 10
force_angle 30
mobility 1
elongation_rate plus barbed 1 2 0
end_state plus barbed
branch_angle 1.22
branch_force_angle 30
branch_segments 1
branch_min_spacing 2
surface_interaction floor front all softwall 20
surface_interaction lid3 front all repel 20 2
surface_interaction lid3 front all branch 3 0.1
surface_interaction walls both all reflect
end_filament_type

random_filament open:a1 3    5 11  1.571
random_filament open:a2 3   10 11  1.571
random_filament open:a3 3   15 11  1.571
random_filament barbed:b1 3  25 11  1.571
random_filament barbed:b2 3  30 11  1.571
random_filament barbed:b3 3  35 11  1.571
random_filament branched:c1 3  45 11  1.571
random_filament branched:c2 3  50 11  1.571
random_filament branched:c3 3  55 11  1.571

cmd E applysurfaceforce lid1 0 -1.5
cmd E applysurfaceforce lid2 0 -1.5
cmd E applysurfaceforce lid3 0 -1.5
cmd A diagnostics surface

end_file
